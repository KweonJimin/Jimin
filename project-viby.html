01 Viby 2026. 01
(Index)
Haptic Wearable — Accessibility, Concept Product

viby is an assistive device designed for deaf and hard-of-hearing audiences. While viewing media content, it translates the director's intended sound effects into Director-Driven Tactile Effects through multiple haptic panels placed on the back of the neck. By delivering auditory intent through touch alongside visual information, viby enables a more immersive and complete media experience using only the eyes and body.
